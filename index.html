Our home

A real house, kept the way a home should be

Horizon Assisted Living is a residential long-term care provider in Glendale, Arizona. We are a full-service assisted living home offering Directed Care services and Memory Care, in a house built for living rather than a facility built for beds.

Our home is modified to contain all of the resources necessary to meet the needs of your loved ones as they continue to change: walk-in showers, accessible bathrooms, wide doorways, and a garden made for slow afternoons outside.

We are dedicated to providing a safe and comfortable home-like environment where our seniors can thrive and continue to live their best lives.

What we offer

Care shaped around each resident

A one size fits all approach does not apply to senior care. Everyone in our home is unique, so each resident's plan of care is personally crafted to suit their individual needs and preferences.

Assisted Living

Personalized support with daily tasks, medication management, and engaging activities, with as much independence as each resident can comfortably keep.

Independent Living

For seniors who manage on their own but want fewer responsibilities: housekeeping, maintenance, and social life handled, with help nearby when needed.

Memory Care

Steady supervision and support for residents with cognitive impairments, with routines and activities tailored to promote cognitive function and quality of life.

Respite Care

Temporary stays that give family caregivers a real rest, knowing their loved one is safe, comfortable, and well looked after in the meantime.

Medication Management

Trained staff give every medication on time and in the correct dosage, with scheduled dosages that keep symptoms managed and families at ease.

Visiting Professionals

Physicians, nurses, physical therapists, podiatrists, labs, X-rays, and even hair dressers come to the house, so residents rarely need to travel for care.
