# Reviews and questions are plain text, with no review or FAQ markup

The home page shows a few real Google reviews as plain quotes and a visible questions block, but the schema carries no `aggregateRating`, `review` or `FAQPage`. Google does not show stars for reviews a business marks up about itself, and per Google's docs (read 11/25/2026) FAQ rich results were removed in May 2026, so the markup would add risk and upkeep for no gain. The words are there for families and for search to read as normal text.
