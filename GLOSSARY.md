# Horizon Assisted Living Home

The words the horizonalh.net site uses for the home, the care it gives, and how families pay. One meaning per word, so every page and listing says the same thing.

## Language

### The home

**Assisted living home**:
What Horizon is: a small licensed home in Glendale, Arizona where up to 10 residents live and get care.
_Avoid_: Facility, nursing home, retirement home, group home, center

**Licensed**:
The home holds an active Arizona state licence. The site says so in words and never prints the licence number.
_Avoid_: Certified, accredited, licence number

### Care services

**Care service**:
A kind of stay or care a family can ask for by name. There are five: Assisted Living, Directed Care, Memory Care, Respite Care, Independent Living. Directed Care and Memory Care are two different things.
_Avoid_: Program, package, level, plan

**Assisted Living**:
The everyday name for help with daily life: bathing, dressing, meals, moving around.
_Avoid_: Personal care, custodial care

**Directed Care**:
Care for someone who can no longer see danger, call for help, speak up for their own needs or make basic choices about their care. It is the Arizona licence word (A.R.S. 36-401), and Horizon is licensed for it. It is not another name for Memory Care.
_Avoid_: Skilled nursing, total care, calling it Memory Care

**Memory Care**:
Services for someone living with dementia or another illness that slowly changes the brain. It is built on top of Directed Care: only a home licensed for Directed Care may offer it (A.R.S. 36-405.03). It is not a licence level of its own. Filip, 11/25/2026: Horizon does both. The site says what the words mean and gives no training or doctor details.
_Avoid_: Dementia unit, Alzheimer's ward, calling it Directed Care

**Respite Care**:
A short stay, so a family caregiver can rest or travel.
_Avoid_: Temporary care, short-term rehab

**Independent Living**:
A stay for a resident who needs little or no hands-on help. Offered, but rare.
_Avoid_: Senior apartment, 55+ living

### Included services

**Included service**:
Something every resident gets as part of care, not something bought on its own. There are two: Medication Management and Visiting Professionals.
_Avoid_: Add-on, extra, amenity

**Medication Management**:
Staff keep, give and track a resident's medicines.
_Avoid_: Med pass, pharmacy service

**Visiting Professionals**:
Outside clinicians who come to the home to see residents.
_Avoid_: On-site doctor, in-house medical staff

### Paying

**Private pay**:
The family or resident pays the monthly price themselves.
_Avoid_: Self-pay, out of pocket, cash

**ALTCS**:
Arizona Long Term Care System, the state Medicaid program that pays for long-term care for people who qualify. Horizon accepts it.
_Avoid_: Medicaid (alone), AHCCCS (alone), state insurance

**Monthly price**:
One price per month that covers the room and furnishings, meals, care, housekeeping and laundry, and Medication Management. The site says what it covers and never prints the amount.
_Avoid_: Rent, fee, rate, tuition
